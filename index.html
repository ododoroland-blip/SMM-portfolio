ABOUT

One person, two brand voices, one calendar.

I'm a graphic designer and social media content lead based in Lagos, currently with Dazzling Group, where I run content for two business lines under one roof: an electronics retail brand and a talent & event agency. That means building separate 30-day content calendars, writing captions that shift tone by platform and audience, and designing every piece of creative that goes out — in Figma, Photoshop, Illustrator, and Canva.

The two case studies below show how that plays out in practice: one calendar built around a product launch, the other around a talent roster. Same planning process, two completely different voices.

BEDAZZLED MART · ELECTRONICS RETAIL

A 30-day calendar built around one product launch.

Bedazzled Mart needed its full electronics catalogue launched with a plan, not a string of one-off posts. I mapped a 30-day content calendar around the online store going live — teaser, spec-focused, lifestyle, and offer posts in sequence — then wrote platform-specific captions per product and prepared e-commerce-ready listings across 231 SKUs (Hisense and LG) so the same launch could run on social and on the store simultaneously.
